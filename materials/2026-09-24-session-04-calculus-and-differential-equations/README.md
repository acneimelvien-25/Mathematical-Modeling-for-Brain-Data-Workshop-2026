## Overview

This session explains how calculus and differential equations describe neural processes that evolve over time.

## Topics

- functions, variables, and parameters in neural models;
- slope, derivatives, and rates of change;
- differential equations as rules for system evolution;
- first-order growth, decay, and relaxation;
- equilibrium points and stability;
- leaky dynamics and return to rest;
- links to membrane-potential and neural-population models;
- numerical approximation and computer simulation; and
- deterministic and stochastic models.

## Learning outcome

Participants should be able to explain rate of change, equilibrium, stability, and time evolution in simple neural models.
