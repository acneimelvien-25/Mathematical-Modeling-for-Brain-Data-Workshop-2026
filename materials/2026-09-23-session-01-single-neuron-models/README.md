# Session 1: Single Neuron Models

- **Date:** 23 September 2026
- **Time:** XX:00-XX:00 UTC
- **Speaker:** AlKilany, Abdal Qader

## Overview

This session introduces the purpose of computational neuron models and shows how biological features can be simplified into mathematical descriptions of membrane potential and spiking.

## Topics

- neuron structure: dendrite, soma, axon, synapse, and spike;
- variables, change over time, and differential-equation intuition;
- membrane potential as a state variable;
- integrate-and-fire and leaky integrate-and-fire models;
- the Hodgkin-Huxley model;
- tradeoffs between simple and realistic models; and
- limitations of single-neuron models.

## Learning outcome

Participants should be able to explain how biological neuron behavior is simplified into mathematical models and how dynamical systems describe changes in membrane potential over time.
