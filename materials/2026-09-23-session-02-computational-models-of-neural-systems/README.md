# Session 2: Computational Models of Neural Systems

- **Date:** 23 September 2026
- **Time:** 18:00-20:00 UTC
- **Speaker:** Anastasia S

## Overview

This session moves from individual neurons to populations and explores how interactions and connectivity produce system-level neural behavior.

## Topics

- why single-neuron models are not enough;
- neural populations and average firing rate;
- mean-field modeling;
- excitatory and inhibitory populations;
- the Wilson-Cowan model;
- equilibria, stability, and oscillations;
- connectivity between populations; and
- emergent behavior in neural systems.

## Learning outcome

Participants should understand how groups of neurons can be modeled mathematically, how excitatory and inhibitory interactions can generate stable or oscillatory activity, and why connectivity matters for emergent brain behavior.
