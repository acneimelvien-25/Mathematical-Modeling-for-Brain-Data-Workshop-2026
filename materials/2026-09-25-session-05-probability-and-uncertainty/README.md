## Overview

This session introduces probability as a language for neural variability, noise, uncertainty, and prediction.

## Topics

- neural variability and random variables;
- Bernoulli, binomial, Gaussian, and Poisson distributions;
- Poisson models for spike counts;
- expectation, variance, and standard deviation;
- inter-spike intervals and spike-count variability;
- biological, measurement, and model noise;
- conditional probability and likelihood; and
- connections to model fitting and prediction.

## Learning outcome

Participants should understand how probability models uncertainty, noise, and variability in neural systems and why neural responses are often treated probabilistically.
